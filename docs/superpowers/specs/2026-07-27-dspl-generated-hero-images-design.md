# DSPL Generated Hero Image System

## Goal

Replace the generic fashion-retail hero photographs on the About, Branding, and E-commerce pages with a coordinated image system that communicates each page's purpose while preserving DSPL's warm institutional identity.

The images must feel editorial and credible, not like generic corporate stock photography or visibly synthetic AI artwork.

## Shared Visual Language

- Photorealistic editorial photography.
- Warm Indian daylight with restrained contrast.
- Warm cream, parchment, walnut, charcoal, muted brass, and small gold accents.
- Realistic materials: paper, cardboard, glass, wood, fabric, and printed packaging.
- Deliberately quiet central area so the existing white and gold hero copy stays readable.
- Subjects and visual detail concentrated toward the outer thirds.
- No gradients, fantasy lighting, excessive luxury styling, or glossy technology clichés.
- No visible company names, fake logos, readable interface text, watermarks, or trademarked products.
- No identifiable generated people.

## Image 1: About DSPL

### Story

An institutional brand-building workspace that suggests research, planning, incubation, and collaboration without pretending to show DSPL's real office or employees.

### Scene

A refined strategy table inside a warm contemporary Indian innovation workspace. The table includes an open neutral notebook, market-research sheets with abstract charts, packaging samples, material swatches, a pen, and a small understated plant. Architectural shelving and frosted glass are softly visible in the background.

### Composition

- Wide environmental photograph.
- No people or only indistinct out-of-focus silhouettes with no identifiable faces.
- Quiet, darker central area for the About heading and introductory copy.
- Workspace details remain visible at the left and right edges.

### Message

Institutional credibility, thoughtful strategy, incubation, and multidisciplinary work.

## Image 2: Branding

### Story

Show the actual work of building a brand rather than a finished fashion store.

### Scene

An art-directed brand studio tabletop with unbranded D2C packaging prototypes, label sheets, typography specimens represented as abstract lines, color swatches, embossing samples, a glass jar, a carton, and folded natural paper.

### Composition

- Warm overhead or three-quarter editorial angle.
- Packaging and tools positioned toward both outer thirds.
- Central region remains low-detail and medium-dark for the headline.
- Avoid fashion handbags, retail shelves, and recognizable consumer brands.

### Message

Identity systems, packaging, storytelling, craft, and commercial readiness.

## Image 3: E-commerce

### Story

Communicate online selling and fulfilment rather than another physical clothing store.

### Scene

A premium but realistic Indian D2C operations environment with neatly arranged unbranded parcels, kraft shipping boxes, a handheld barcode scanner, packing materials, a smartphone and laptop showing abstract non-readable commerce shapes, and organized shelving in the background.

### Composition

- Wide warehouse-studio hybrid, clean but actively used.
- Operational details sit primarily on the left and right.
- Dark, quiet central zone supports the existing E-commerce text.
- No courier logos, marketplace logos, readable dashboards, currency values, or customer information.

### Message

Digital storefronts, orders, payments, fulfilment, reliability, and scalable operations.

## Asset Strategy

1. Generate one 2048 x 1152 landscape candidate for each page.
2. Review the three candidates together for lighting, color, realism, and differentiation.
3. Revise only the selected candidate when a targeted change is needed.
4. After approval, create a mobile companion version for each selected image rather than relying on an unpredictable center crop.
5. Save approved files as WebP assets with descriptive versioned names; do not overwrite the current images until final approval.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Each image communicates its page without reading the headline.
- All three images unmistakably belong to the same website.
- Branding and E-commerce do not resemble the same physical retail category.
- About feels credible without inventing real DSPL people, offices, awards, or partnerships.
- Existing white and gold hero text remains legible at desktop and mobile breakpoints.
- Images contain no malformed hands, impossible objects, fake readable text, watermarks, or recognizable third-party branding.
- The final result supports DSPL's warm cream, charcoal, and restrained gold design system.

## Scope

This phase covers image generation and selection for About, Branding, and E-commerce only. It does not alter page copy, layout, logos, or production hosting.
